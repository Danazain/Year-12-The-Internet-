Mission 5 · Buffering calculator

Assume binary rates: incoming 1.5 Mib/s, playback 600 Kib/s, starting buffer 200 KiB. How much data remains after 2 seconds? (1 byte = 8 bits, 1 MiB = 1024 KiB.)

Incoming = 1.5 × 1024 ÷ 8 = 192 KiB/s. Outgoing = 600 ÷ 8 = 75 KiB/s. Net = 117 KiB/s. After 2 seconds: 200 + (117 × 2) = 434 KiB.
